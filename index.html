Our Story

Mermaid Loops was born out of a profound love for the timeless art of crochet. What started as making cozy sweaters for family members quickly blossomed into a mission to share sustainable, slow-fashion warmth with the world.

We believe in the magic of handmade goods. In a world of fast fashion, every piece we create is an heirloom — designed to last, comfort, and bring joy.
